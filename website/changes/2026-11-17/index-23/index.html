Prerequisites

To use Advanced Analytics features,features in endpoint analytics, devices must meet the endpoint analytics prerequisites and you must configure endpoint analytics in your tenant.

This section details additional prerequisites specific to Advanced Analytics. Certain features may have their own additional prerequisites; see the individual feature articles for more information.

It might take up to 48 hours after you buy licenses or start a trial to see Advanced Analytics features in your tenant.

For the extraendpoint analytics reports and capabilities on Windows devices:

  • Devices must be enrolled in Intune and onboarded to endpoint analytics.
  • Device query for multiple devices requires a properties catalog policy to be configured and deployed.
  • Additional capabilities:
    • Device query for multiple devices under the Devices node in the Intune admin center
    • STIG audit baseline to assess Windows device compliance against Department of Defense (DoD) Security Technical Implementation Guide requirements
    • CIS audit baseline (preview) to assess Windows 11 device configurations against the Center for Internet Security (CIS) benchmark
