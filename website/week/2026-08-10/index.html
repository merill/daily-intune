Week in brief

Intune plans iOS/iPadOS 18 and macOS 15 floors as sync guidance broadens

Two Message Center plans dominate the week: Intune will require iOS/iPadOS 18 or later after Apple releases iOS/iPadOS 27, and will support macOS 15 or later after macOS Golden Gate 27 releases later this year. Documentation updates mainly clarify operational guidance around device sync, registry-inventory risk, and Android Management API reporting. Two endpoint-security page edits changed only metadata dates and show no product or administrator impact.

  • Intune · App management
    iOS/iPadOS 18 becomes the planned Intune support floor

    A Message Center Plan for Change says Intune will require iOS/iPadOS 18 or higher after Apple releases iOS/iPadOS 27 later this year. Administrators should check device compatibility and Intune reports; userless devices enrolled through Automated Device Enrollment have specific OS-version requirements. Intune controls can be used to manage OS versions.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    macOS 15 becomes the planned support floor

    After macOS Golden Gate 27 releases this year, Intune will support macOS 15 and later. Devices running macOS 14.x or below will remain enrolled, but new enrollments on those versions will not be allowed. Organizations should identify and upgrade affected devices before the change.

  • Intune · General
    Sync guidance documents broader processing and cross-platform status tracking

    The updated Sync guidance says selecting Sync triggers multiple workloads, including policy processing, app state updates, scripts, and remediations. Progress can be monitored in the Device sync status tab, and the guidance applies to iOS/iPadOS and Windows devices. The documented improvements require the Preview new device view toggle in the Intune admin center.

  • Intune · Device configuration
    Registry inventory guidance adds an explicit sensitive-configuration risk

    The Collect Device Properties update states that registry key inventory uses existing Device Inventory permissions and may expose sensitive device configuration information. Microsoft identifies this as an accepted risk and calls for review of the related security and privacy implications.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    Android Management API guidance specifies the report to use

    The updated overview directs administrators to the Personal Devices on Android Management API report under Devices > Monitor. It also clarifies that this reporting is not shown in the policy’s device assignment status or in the device configuration tab.

For Intune administrators

Prioritize compatibility inventory and upgrade planning for affected iOS/iPadOS and macOS devices, including the specific requirements for userless devices enrolled through Automated Device Enrollment. For the documented sync improvements, enable Preview new device view and monitor the Device sync status tab. Review access to registry inventory and its security and privacy implications, and use the named Android Management API report under Devices > Monitor. No action is indicated for the metadata-only endpoint-security updates.
