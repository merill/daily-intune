Add Managed Google Play

18 November 2026Doc update+6 / −6 lines

In brief

The instructions now explicitly identify Android as the platform, the applicable category, and the Managed Google Play app type.

What Intune admins need to know

Administrators can use the named Platform, Category, and App type fields when following the app-addition steps.

This summary was assembled from the tracked documentation change. Verify important details in the full Microsoft Learn article.
