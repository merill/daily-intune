Turn off Battery Optimization in Company Portal App for Android - Microsoft Intune | Microsoft Learn

14 November 2026Doc update+8 / −8 lines

In brief

The Company Portal Android help page was refreshed with updated wording, a new explanation of battery optimization’s effect on background operation, and revised step instructions using “select” instead of “tap.”

What Intune admins need to know

Administrators can use the updated guidance when helping users keep Company Portal running in the background; no administrator changes are required.

This summary was assembled from the tracked documentation change. Verify important details in the full Microsoft Learn article.

Turn off battery optimizationBattery Optimization in Company Portal appApp for Android - Microsoft Intune | Microsoft Learn

Applies to Company Portal app for Android

To ensure that your work or school device stays up-to-date and connected, turn off battery optimization in the Company Portal app for Android.

  1. Sign in to the Company Portal app with your work or school account.
  2. Open the menu and tapselect Settings.
  3. Scroll to Battery Optimization and tapselect TURN OFF.
  4. TapSelect Allow to confirm that you want to turn battery optimization off.
