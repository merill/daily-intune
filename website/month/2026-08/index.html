Month in brief

Intune plans iOS 18 and macOS 15 floors as device page shifts

August’s most consequential Intune developments were two planned Apple platform changes and a scheduled admin-center interface change. Intune will require iOS/iPadOS 18 or higher after Apple releases iOS/iPadOS 27 later this year, while macOS 15 or later will be supported after macOS Golden Gate 27; existing macOS 14.x-and-below devices remain enrolled, but new enrollments on those versions will be blocked. The consolidated single-device page is planned to replace the legacy default in September 2609. Supporting guidance also defines MDM Firewall and ASR reporting limits and cumulative tenant-wide device-action quotas.

  • Intune · App management
    iOS/iPadOS 18 becomes Intune’s planned platform floor

    A Message Center plan says Intune will require iOS/iPadOS 18 or higher after Apple releases iOS/iPadOS 27 later this year. Microsoft directs organizations to check device compatibility and Intune reports; userless devices enrolled through Automated Device Enrollment have specific OS-version requirements.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    macOS 15 becomes the supported floor after macOS Golden Gate 27

    After the macOS Golden Gate 27 release this year, Intune will support macOS 15 and later. Existing devices on macOS 14.x or below remain enrolled, but new enrollments on those versions won’t be allowed.

  • Intune · Endpoint analytics
    The consolidated single-device page becomes default in September 2609

    The planned September 2609 release will make Intune’s new single-device page the default view and replace the legacy page. The consolidated view retains all management features, but administrators should update internal documentation and familiarize staff with the new interface.

  • Intune · Device security
    MDM Firewall and ASR reporting is limited to policy level

    For Firewall Rules and Attack Surface Reduction (ASR) Rules policies managed through MDM, reporting is limited to policy-level status and doesn’t include per-setting reporting statuses. The updated guidance says devices must be enrolled with Defender for Endpoint security settings management to view per-setting status for those policies.

  • Intune · Android
    Device-action guidance sets cumulative tenant-wide daily quotas

    The updated Intune guidance lists daily tenant-wide limits of 500 Wipe actions and 1,000 each for Retire and Delete. Individual, bulk, and Microsoft Graph submissions count toward the same cumulative totals.

For Intune administrators

Inventory iOS/iPadOS and macOS versions, including userless Automated Device Enrollment scenarios with their stated OS requirements, and plan upgrades before the platform changes. Update administrator runbooks and training for the September 2609 device-page transition. Set expectations that MDM-managed Firewall Rules and Attack Surface Reduction Rules provide policy-level rather than per-setting reporting unless devices use Defender for Endpoint security settings management. Schedule Wipe, Retire, and Delete operations within the documented cumulative tenant limits of 500, 1,000, and 1,000 actions per day respectively.
