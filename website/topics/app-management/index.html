18 November 2026

App management

The instructions now specify selecting Android as the platform, Built-in app as the category, and Built-in app as the app type for Android apps. The iOS/iPadOS selection path remains listed under Other.

18 November 2026

17 November 2026

Microsoft Purview DLP: Improved setup and Intune multi-admin approval support for unmanaged apps in Edge for Business

Message CenterMC1489971 on mc.merill.net ↗Stay informed
App management

Microsoft Purview DLP will enhance setup and activation for unmanaged apps in Edge for Business, supporting Intune multi-admin approval with clearer status, error reporting, and task coordination. Rollout starts January 2027, requiring no prior action; existing policies remain unchanged and Intune approvals continue as before.
