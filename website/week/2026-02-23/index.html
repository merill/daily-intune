Week in brief

Apple update retirement, SCEP allowlists, and filter mappings reshape Intune administration

The week’s consequential Intune changes center on Apple management, authorization, certificate security, and query semantics. Service release 2602 adds assignment filters for Apple declarative device management configurations and extends multi-administrator approval to Settings Catalog device-configuration and compliance policies. Intune also says it will soon stop creating legacy Apple software-update policies after Apple deprecated the relevant commands and payloads for iOS 26, iPadOS 26, and macOS 26. Separately, updated Certificate Connector guidance records SCEP validation blocking unknown certificate extensions from version 6.2510.3.2002. Much of the remaining evidence is documentation maintenance or clarification, including privacy/data-sharing pages, tenant-status and cleanup guidance, and Windows diagnostic-data procedures; those additions do not by themselves establish new product launches.

  • Intune · Fundamentals
    Apple DDM filters arrive as legacy software-update policies approach retirement

    Service release 2602 allows assignment filters on Apple declarative device management configurations, including software updates, for iOS/iPadOS. Separately, Apple deprecated legacy MDM software-update commands and payloads with iOS 26, iPadOS 26, and macOS 26; Intune says it will soon end support for creating legacy iOS/iPadOS and macOS software-update policies. No retirement date is supplied.

  • Intune · Fundamentals
    Multi-administrator approval expands to compliance and Settings Catalog policies

    Service release 2602 extends multi-administrator approval to device-configuration policies created through Settings Catalog and to device-compliance policies. When enabled, creating, editing, or deleting one of these policies requires approval from a second administrator before the change takes effect.

  • Intune · Device security
    Certificate Connector guidance records a six-OID SCEP allowlist

    Security behavior documented for Intune Certificate Connector version 6.2510.3.2002 blocks unknown certificate extensions during SCEP validation. The allowed and enforced OIDs are `2.5.29.19` Basic Constraints, `2.5.29.14` Subject Key Identifier, `1.3.6.1.4.1.311.21.8` Certificate Authority Version, `1.3.6.1.4.1.311.20.2` Certificate Template Name, `2.5.29.1` Authority Identifier (Deprecated), and `2.5.29.3` Certificate Policies (Deprecated).

  • Intune · Android
    Assignment filters retire broad Android and iOS management values

    The `Android Enterprise` and iOS/iPadOS `Managed` values are being replaced and will be removed in a future update, with no ETA. Existing filters are automatically mapped: `Android Enterprise` maps to the documented corporate-owned and personally-owned Android management types, while `Managed` maps to the four documented Automated Device Enrollment, Account Driven User Enrollment, and Company Portal/Web Enrollment types.   • Intune · Fundamentals
    Device Query adds semi and anti joins but rejects older syntax

    Advanced Analytics device queries now support `leftsemi`, `rightsemi`, `leftanti`, and `rightanti` joins. Joins using `on Device.DeviceId` are no longer supported; queries should use `on Device` or omit the `on` clause when joining on the device entity. Operators such as `distinct`, `summarize`, and `order by` must reference a specific device property rather than `Device`, while device-related results now expose clickable device links.

For Intune administrators

Inventory legacy Apple software-update policies and plan around the stated retirement; no retirement date is supplied. Test DDM assignment filters and confirm second-administrator approval workflows where enabled. Review SCEP certificate templates and requests against the documented six-OID allowlist. Recheck assignment filters using the deprecated management values, especially where devices lack Microsoft Entra registration or a Microsoft Entra Device ID, and revise device queries that use unsupported join or device references. If dependent Windows reporting or update-alert features are important, note that the Windows diagnostic-data toggle defaults to Off and disabling it disables dependent Intune features; license attestation confirms tenant entitlement rather than licensing individual
