Day in brief

iOS Company Portal minimum rises in December as Purview DLP adds Intune approvals

Two Message Center notices carry the main operational weight this period. The iOS/iPadOS Company Portal minimum will become version 5.2608.0 in early December, while Purview DLP will add Intune multi-admin approval support for unmanaged apps in Edge for Business in late November. The remaining changes are primarily documentation: new MDM partner eligibility guidance, a proxy-scope clarification, Managed Home Screen JSON guidance, and wording or formatting refreshes for enrollment and Company Portal articles.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    iOS/iPadOS Company Portal minimum version moves to v5.2608.0

    Starting in early December, v5.2608.0 will be the minimum supported iOS/iPadOS Company Portal version for new Apple operating-system releases. Most users update automatically or receive a prompt, but device restrictions that block app installation may require a manual update process.

  • Intune · App management
    Purview DLP adds Intune multi-admin approval support for unmanaged Edge apps

    Starting in late November 2026, setup and activation for unmanaged apps in Edge for Business will gain Intune multi-admin approval support, clearer status information, detailed errors, and task coordination between Purview and Intune administrators. Existing policies do not require migration.

  • Intune · Fundamentals
    Setup Connector guidance separates connector proxy settings from Windows WinHTTP

    The article now states that Certificate Connector wizard proxy settings apply only to connector services, not the system-wide Windows WinHTTP proxy. It also documents how to view, set, verify, and reset a static WinHTTP proxy.

  • Intune · App management
    Managed Home Screen guidance adds JSON-based app exclusion direction

    The table entry for “Exclude these apps from the silence setting” now points administrators to the Enter JSON Data section and explains the exclusion behavior when Managed Home Screen requires authentication.

  • Intune · Device security
    New guidance documents eligibility and application steps for MDM partners

    The new partner article describes the application process and requires eligible solutions to support MDM, user-based compliance, Microsoft Entra ID, and Android, iOS/iPadOS, or macOS. MDM vendors can use it to assess eligibility and apply.
