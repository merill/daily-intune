Day in brief

Windows update and Edge for Business guidance are reorganized into task-focused guides

The 55 changes are chiefly a documentation reorganization. Windows update policy material is renamed and split into discrete driver, feature, and update-ring guides, while the Microsoft Edge for Business data-security sequence receives new end-user-experience and troubleshooting steps and retires obsolete pages.

  • Intune · App management
    Microsoft Edge for Business data-security troubleshooting is re-sequenced

    A new troubleshooting step replaces the older end-of-sequence page as part of the Edge for Business data-security documentation refresh. The companion security-baseline and end-user-experience pages are retired or renumbered, so internal documentation should follow the new sequence.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Windows driver, feature, and update-ring policies move to focused guides

    New task-focused policy pages replace older plural-named documentation, separating configuration guidance for driver updates, feature updates, and update-ring settings. This is primarily an information-architecture change, but it changes the canonical links administrators should use.

  • Endpoint Privilege Management · Device security
    Centralized Admin tasks now include Device Offboarding work

    The Admin tasks documentation adds Device Offboarding tasks and points to their current admin-center location. The unified view is described as covering Device Offboarding, Endpoint Privilege Management, and other administrative work, governed by Organization Read permission.
