Month in brief

Intune plans higher Apple OS floors as Sync preview guidance broadens device scope

August was update-heavy rather than launch-heavy: 41 Learn pages were updated, with no new or removed records, alongside two Message Center Plan for Change notices. The most consequential items are future iOS/iPadOS and macOS support floors, clarified limits on MDM Firewall and ASR reporting, preview Sync guidance, and registry-inventory security disclosure. Most other edits involved links, metadata, labels, examples, or scope clarification. No supplied item establishes a general-availability launch or product retirement.

  • Intune · App management
    iOS/iPadOS support floor moves to 18 after iOS/iPadOS 27

    Plan for Change: after Apple releases iOS/iPadOS 27 later this year, Intune will require iOS/iPadOS 18 or higher. Check device compatibility and Intune reports; userless devices enrolled through Automated Device Enrollment have separate OS requirements. This is a future support-policy change, not a current general-availability feature announcement.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    macOS 15 becomes the future enrollment floor after Golden Gate 27

    Plan for Change: after macOS Golden Gate 27 releases, Intune will support macOS 15 and later. Devices already enrolled on macOS 14.x or below remain enrolled, but new enrollments on those versions will not be allowed. This is a planned enrollment-behavior change; no exact enforcement date is supplied.

  • Intune · Device security
    MDM Firewall and ASR policies remain limited to policy-level reporting

    Documentation clarification: most Endpoint Security policies have per-setting reporting, but Firewall Rules and Attack Surface Reduction (ASR) Rules managed through MDM expose only policy-level status. Per-setting status requires devices to be enrolled with Defender for Endpoint security settings management. The change clarifies reporting expectations rather than policy enforcement.

  • Intune · Fundamentals
    Sync preview guidance now covers Windows and iOS/iPadOS devices

    Documentation clarification: the Sync behavior and Device sync status tab are now described as applying to Windows and iOS/iPadOS, rather than Windows only. Administrators must enable the Preview new device view toggle to see the described improvements. This is preview guidance, not evidence of general availability.

  • Intune · Device configuration
    Registry inventory guidance flags sensitive configuration exposure

    Security and privacy guidance now states that collected registry key inventory is accessible through existing Device Inventory permissions and may expose sensitive device configuration information. The guidance identifies this as an accepted risk and calls for reviewing the security and privacy implications. It does not show that permissions or inventory behavior changed.

For Intune administrators

Begin Apple compatibility and enrollment inventory work, especially for iOS/iPadOS and macOS devices approaching the announced floors; the notices provide post-release timing but no exact enforcement date. Set accurate reporting expectations for MDM Firewall Rules and ASR Rules, enable Preview new device view when evaluating the documented Sync improvements, and review which administrators can access registry inventory through existing Device Inventory permissions. Mechanical documentation edits generally require no tenant action.
