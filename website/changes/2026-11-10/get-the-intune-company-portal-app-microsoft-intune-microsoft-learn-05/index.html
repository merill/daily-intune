Sign in with certificate

You'll only see this sign-in option only if your organization allows certificate-based authentication and you have a certificate available to use.

  1. Open the Company Portal app on your device.
  2. Enter the email address associated with your work or school account and then tapselect Next.
  3. TapSelect Sign in with a certificate.
  4. TapSelect Continue to use the certificate.
  5. Wait while the app verifies your certificate. When done, you can access the features in the app and your organization's resources.
