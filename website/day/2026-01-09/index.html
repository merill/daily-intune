Day in brief

Windows update guidance adds rollout controls, Hotpatch prerequisites, and Autopatch ring rules

The period is led by a focused expansion of Intune guidance for Windows servicing. New feature- and quality-update policy pages cover policy assignment, rollout timing, safeguard holds, Hotpatch, expedited updates, and conflicting update settings; related ring guidance says Autopatch may create and maintain rings. Mobile Threat Defense guidance also documents optional iOS/iPadOS Certificate Sync. Many of the other edits are reviewer metadata, formatting, or terminology changes.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Feature update policy guidance now covers rollout timing and safeguard holds

    The new Windows feature update policies page explains how to create and assign policies, configure rollout timing, manage optional or required updates, and understand safeguard holds and multi-policy behavior. It gives administrators a documented workflow for deploying supported Windows versions and anticipating device and user update behavior.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Quality update guidance spells out Hotpatch and expedited-update prerequisites

    The new Windows quality updates policy page adds prerequisites and configuration guidance for Hotpatch and expedited updates, including update ring and Group Policy settings that can interfere with installation. Administrators should validate those settings before deployment.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Expedited-update guidance records a Windows 11 24H2 KB limitation

    The expedited updates article now states that Windows 11 version 24H2 and later cannot apply KB 4023057. It also reorganizes prerequisite material and separates Update Health Tools guidance, giving servicing teams a specific compatibility constraint for update planning.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Autopatch guidance warns against custom rings on managed devices

    The Windows Update rings policy guidance says Windows Autopatch may create and maintain rings, and that administrators typically shouldn't assign custom rings to Autopatch-managed devices. Review ring assignments where Autopatch coordinates updates.

  • Intune · Device security
    Mobile Threat Defense guidance adds optional iOS/iPadOS Certificate Sync detail

    The updated Mobile Threat Defense guidance documents optional Certificate Sync through MTD connectors for iOS/iPadOS. It covers certificate inventory sharing for corporate and personally owned devices, the certificate data sent, and how connector status affects synchronization requests. To share inventory, enable Certificate Sync in the connector settings.
