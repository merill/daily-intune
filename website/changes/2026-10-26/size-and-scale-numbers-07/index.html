Size And Scale Numbers

26 October 2026Feature updateAction required+26 / −8 lines

In brief

Configuration Manager 2609 and later, and 2603 with KB39169655, use Small, Medium, and Large CMG tiers with new VM sizes. Existing deployments are automatically mapped to the corresponding tiers, while client connection limits remain unchanged. Older versions retain the previous sizes and tiers.

What Intune admins need to know

Before updating, verify the new VM sizes are available in the Azure region and that the required vCPU quotas have sufficient headroom for temporary instances.

This summary was assembled from the tracked documentation change. Verify important details in the full Microsoft Learn article.

    **Version 2609 and later, and version 2603 with the update rollup (KB39169655)**

    | CMG tier | VM size | vCPU | Memory | Simultaneous client connections per VM instance |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Small | Standard\_D2ads\_v6 | 2 | 8 GiB | 10 |
    | Medium | Standard\_D4ads\_v6 | 4 | 16 GiB | 6,000 |
    | Large | Standard\_D8ads\_v6 | 8 | 32 GiB | 10,000 |

    When you update to version 2609 or install the [version 2603 update rollup (KB39169655)](../../../hotfix/2603/39169655), existing deployments are automatically migrated: Lab to Small, Standard to Medium, and Large to Large. The client connection limits for each tier remain the same.

    Important

    Before updating, check that the new VM sizes are available in your Azure region and that both the **standard Dadv6 Family vCPUs** and **Total Regional vCPUs** quotas have enough available quota, including headroom for temporary instances during upgrade or maintenance. For details, see [Check CMG capacity and vCPU quotas before upgrading](../changes/whats-new-version-2609#check-cmg-capacity-and-vcpu-quotas-before-upgrading). Upgrade the Configuration Manager console after installing the update so that it displays the new VM sizes correctly.

    | Standard | Standard\_A2\_v2 | 2 | 4 GiB | 6,000 |
    | Large | Standard\_A4\_v2 | 4 | 8 GiB | 10,000 |

The B2s VMs are low cost and low performing. The Configuration Manager technical preview branch only supports 10 clients, which is why this sizetier supports that number of clients.
