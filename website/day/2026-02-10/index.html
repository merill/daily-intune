Day in brief

Intune will exclude long-inactive devices from reports starting in late February

The clearest service-level change is a planned reporting adjustment: beginning in late February 2026, devices inactive for 12 months or more will be excluded from Intune reports, which may reduce reported counts. The period also brought substantial Apple configuration-documentation changes, including new Wi-Fi, iCloud, and Kerberos guidance alongside the removal of ten standalone Apple reference pages, plus a Configuration Manager port correction.

  • Intune · Endpoint analytics
    Inactive devices will drop out of Intune reports in late February

    Starting in late February 2026, Intune will exclude devices inactive for 12 months or more from reports. Counts may decrease because reports will represent active devices; devices must check in to remain included, and the message states that no other action is required.

  • Intune · Apple
    Standalone iOS custom-profile guidance was removed

    The article covering custom profile creation with Apple Configurator or Profile Manager, importing profiles into Intune, and assigning them to devices was deleted. Administrators relying on that workflow can no longer use that Microsoft Learn reference at its published location.

  • Intune · Apple
    Apple Wi-Fi guidance adds enrollment and certificate-authentication detail

    The page now states that the settings are available for all enrollment types and adds guidance for selecting SCEP or PKCS certificates and configuring EAP outer identity on iOS/iPadOS and macOS.

  • Intune · Apple
    Apple restrictions now document Block iCloud desktop and documents sync

    The documented setting uses Yes to block iCloud from syncing desktop and documents folders, while Not configured leaves the operating system behavior unchanged.

  • Configuration Manager · General
    Configuration Manager ports guidance now lists SQL Server Browser on UDP 1434

    The ports page lists SQL Server Browser port 1434 under UDP instead of TCP. Configuration Manager firewall reviews should account for SQL Server Browser traffic on UDP 1434.
