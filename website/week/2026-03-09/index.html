Week in brief

Security policy maintenance and Autopatch Hotpatch readiness dominate the week

The week records 82 changes, including major refreshes to ASR, antivirus, EDR, firewall, and baseline references. Two Message Center notices publish the March roadmap and the May default enablement of Autopatch Hotpatch.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    Autopatch plans Hotpatch as the eligible-device default

    From May 2026, Autopatch will enable Hotpatch by default for eligible devices; administrators gain an opt-out in April and should validate prerequisites including VBS.

  • Intune · Device security
    Endpoint-security policy references are comprehensively refreshed

    ASR, antivirus, EDR, firewall, and tenant-attach firewall setting guidance are updated across the week.

  • Intune · General
    March In Development roadmap is published

    The roadmap provides the current upcoming-feature list for organizational readiness planning.

For Intune administrators

Start Hotpatch readiness and opt-out decisions now, review endpoints for VBS and eligibility, and update ASR, firewall, EDR, and antivirus runbooks from the refreshed references. Include the March roadmap in change-planning cadence.

Plan for Change: Windows Autopatch is enabling hotpatch updates by default

New

Starting May 2026, Windows Autopatch will enable hotpatch security updates by default for eligible Intune devices, speeding up security without restarts. An opt-out setting will be available from April 2026. Devices must meet prerequisites like enabling Virtualization-based Security to receive hotpatches.
