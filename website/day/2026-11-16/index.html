Day in brief

Company Portal minimum rises to v5.2608.0 on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS in February 2027

The period’s clearest administrator deadline is the February 2027 Company Portal minimum. Alongside that forward-looking change, Intune guidance adds concrete Android Enterprise Local Actions permissions, records macOS Platform SSO enrollment prerequisites, and documents Windows targeting and protected-app additions. Windows Autopilot material also clarifies recovery-key access for reused devices; several other edits are terminology or reference updates.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    Company Portal minimum version moves to v5.2608.0 in February 2027

    Starting in February 2027, the minimum iOS/iPadOS and macOS Company Portal version will be v5.2608.0. Most users update automatically, but users whose app installation is blocked may need a manual update; Microsoft advises informing helpdesk staff and pushing updates where device restrictions are enabled.

  • Intune · Android
    Android Local Actions guidance adds app permissions and deployment limits

    The OEMConfig guidance now covers permissions for both Managed Home Screen and the Intune app in Local Actions scenarios. It adds package names, permission details, prerequisites, supported OEMs, role requirements, and a 500-KB profile limit; when Local Actions and Managed Home Screen are used together, overlay permission is required for both apps.

  • Intune · General
    Platform SSO enrollment guidance now covers macOS ADE on macOS 26+

    The archive now documents Platform SSO registration during macOS Automated Device Enrollment for macOS 26 and later when Company Portal 5.2604.0 or later is deployed. The documented setup uses specified Settings Catalog and ADE policies.

  • Intune · General
    Windows script targeting and protected-app references expand

    The November 16 service-release index calls out PowerShell script group exclusions and built-in group targeting on Windows. It also lists 10 newly available protected apps: EnGlobe Mobile, Everbridge 360, Hypergate Files for Intune, Newcode, Ramp, Reliaquest GreyMatter, Rogo AI, Rogo AI (Intune Wrapped), ShareCRM, and Zoho Mail - Intune.

  • Windows Autopilot · Device enrollment
    Windows Autopilot guidance distinguishes recovery-key access by ownership

    For affected reused Windows Autopilot devices, non-owner IT administrators retain recovery-key access. An administrator who owns the device faces the same BitLocker self-service restriction as a user, even with administrative permissions, and must contact IT assistance for the recovery key.
