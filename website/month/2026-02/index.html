Month in brief

February sharpened Intune operational governance, reporting quality, and policy foundations

February recorded 675 changes and three Message Center announcements. It consolidated Apple profile guidance, refreshed RBAC and endpoint-security references, added a major privacy and governance documentation set, clarified Cloud PKI CA continuity, and announced reporting treatment for devices inactive at least 12 months.

  • Intune · Device security
    Endpoint security guidance is extensively refreshed

    The core Intune device-protection reference receives a substantial revision and remains the foundation for endpoint-security policy design.

  • Intune · Endpoint analytics
    Inactive devices are excluded from Intune reporting

    Beginning in late February, reports exclude devices inactive for 12 months or more, improving active-fleet accuracy while potentially lowering reported counts.

  • Intune · Compliance
    Privacy and regulatory documentation is reorganized

    A broad new documentation set provides current descriptions of Intune data handling, secure sharing, partner data flows, and compliance alignment.

For Intune administrators

Prioritize updating privacy and regulatory documentation, Apple configuration runbooks, and custom-role audits. Set expectations for reporting-count changes in late February, preserve Cloud PKI CA replacement readiness, and use the refreshed Endpoint security and Autopilot guidance as support baselines.
