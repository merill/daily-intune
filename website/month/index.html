Month in brief

Future Apple OS minimums, DDM guidance, and unattended Remote Help shape Intune planning

August was update-heavy, but the consequential items are concrete: Intune plans to raise iOS/iPadOS and macOS support or enrollment floors later this year; a new Apple DDM App Settings article covers OS 27+ app and binary controls and the iOS 27 migration from Restrictions-profile app-launch settings; and Remote Help guidance details unattended Windows deployment. Firewall guidance also clarifies when per-setting status is available, while the new single-device page is slated to replace the legacy view as the default in September 2609.

  • Intune · App management
    Intune will require iOS/iPadOS 18 or later after iOS/iPadOS 27

    A Message Center plan says Intune will require iOS/iPadOS 18 or higher after Apple releases iOS/iPadOS 27 later this year. It calls for compatibility checks and Intune report review; userless devices enrolled through Automated Device Enrollment have separate OS requirements.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    macOS 15 becomes the future Intune minimum for new enrollments

    After macOS Golden Gate 27 releases later this year, Intune will support macOS 15 and later. Devices on macOS 14.x or below remain enrolled, but new enrollments on those versions will not be allowed.

  • Intune · Apple
    Apple DDM App Settings guidance covers app and binary controls on OS 27 devices

    A new Intune article documents Apple DDM App Settings in the Settings catalog for supervised iOS/iPadOS 27+ and macOS 27+ devices, including allowed or denied apps and binaries and managed-app allowances. Existing iOS app-launch restrictions in Restrictions profiles must be migrated because those settings are deprecated in iOS 27.

  • Remote Help · Android
    Remote Help guidance specifies unattended Windows deployment prerequisites

    The updated deployment guidance covers Windows unattended support and specifies required custom Intune-role permissions, the Azure Virtual Desktop agent and bootloader prerequisites, and Win32 deployment steps. The agent must be deployed before the bootloader, with the bootloader configured to depend on the agent.

  • Intune · Device security
    MDM Firewall Rules and ASR Rules have policy-level reporting limits

    The Firewall guidance states that per-setting reporting is available for most Endpoint Security policies, but Firewall Rules and Attack Surface Reduction (ASR) Rules policies managed through MDM provide only policy-level status, not per-setting statuses. Per-setting status requires devices enrolled with Defender for Endpoint security settings management.

For Intune administrators

Inventory iOS/iPadOS and macOS fleets and plan upgrades against the later Apple releases, including the separate OS requirements for userless Automated Device Enrollment devices. Migrate iOS Restrictions-profile app-launch controls to Apple DDM App Settings for iOS 27. For unattended Remote Help, create the required custom role and deploy the Azure Virtual Desktop agent before the bootloader, with the bootloader dependent on the agent. Set reporting expectations for MDM Firewall Rules and ASR Rules to policy-level unless devices use Defender for Endpoint security settings management. Update runbooks and brief administrators before the September 2609 device-page change.
