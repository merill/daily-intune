Day in brief

Autopilot device preparation guidance details physical Windows 11 association before enrollment

The two updates concern one Windows Autopilot device-association capability. The guidance describes associating physical Windows 11 devices with an organization before enrollment, marking them corporate-owned and enabling device-targeted policies and naming. The related entry was moved from the general What's New page to Device preparation What's New.

  • Windows Autopilot · Device enrollment
    Physical Windows 11 devices can be associated before enrollment

    The Device preparation What's New guidance describes associating physical Windows 11 devices with an organization before enrollment. The associated devices are marked corporate-owned and can receive device-targeted policies and naming.

  • Windows Autopilot · Device enrollment
    Device-association guidance moves to Device preparation What's New

    The device-association entry was removed from the general Windows Autopilot What's New page and moved to Device preparation What's New. The page date was changed to June 18, 2026, so administrators tracking this capability should consult the Device preparation page.
