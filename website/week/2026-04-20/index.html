Week in brief

macOS ADE guidance adds pre-enrollment Platform SSO setup

This was a documentation-heavy Intune week: 40 new, 269 updated, and 45 removed items, with no Message Center entries. The most consequential cluster concerned Apple enrollment: the ADE material now documents macOS Platform SSO sequencing, explicit tvOS and visionOS coverage with licensing guidance, and Apple account service-access prerequisites. Separate security guidance changes replace Ubuntu 22.04/24.04 with 24.04/26.04 in a compliance-policy page and change the Edge for Business threat-level example from Medium to Secured. The evidence identifies documentation changes, not a product launch, preview, general-availability milestone, tenant-wide policy migration, or capability retirement.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    Platform SSO is now documented as a macOS ADE prerequisite

    Updated Intune macOS ADE guidance adds a Configure platform SSO section. It says that, if Microsoft Entra SSO should be available during Setup Assistant, administrators must create the platform SSO policy before devices enroll. The page says ADE deploys that policy to enrolling Macs, enabling organizational-credential sign-in during setup and access to Microsoft Entra-protected apps and resources afterward. This is a procedure and prerequisite documentation change, not a supplied GA announcement.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    Apple ADE guidance now lists tvOS and visionOS with Plan 2 licensing

    The refreshed Apple mobile ADE overview lists iOS/iPadOS, tvOS, and visionOS and says administrators can create, edit, delete, and assign platform-specific ADE policies. It also states that Intune Plan 2 is required for tvOS and visionOS device management, while Plan 1 is the minimum requirement for iOS/iPadOS. The record shows expanded documentation coverage; it does not label the scope as preview or general availability.

  • Intune · Device enrollment
    New Apple account service-access guidance keeps configuration in Apple portals

    A new Intune how-to explains that Apple account service-access settings are configured in Apple Business or Apple School Manager, not directly in Intune; Intune issues the management token Apple uses during device activation. The guidance applies to organization-owned devices enrolled through ADE, with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later or macOS 14 or later. It warns that changing the settings can automatically sign out accounts on devices that no longer meet the requirements.

  • Intune · Device security
    Linux compliance guidance replaces Ubuntu 22.04 with 26.04

    In the Create Policy guidance, the Ubuntu Desktop version list changes from 22.04 LTS or 24.04 LTS to 24.04 LTS or 26.04 LTS. This is a documentation value replacement; the diff does not show an automated change to existing compliance policies or establish changed service behavior.

  • Intune · App management
    Edge for Business app protection guidance raises the threat-level example

    The Step 2 setup guidance changes Max allowed device threat level from Medium / Block access to Secured / Block access; the Block access action remains unchanged. Treat this as an updated configuration example and compare it with existing policies before changing a tenant baseline.

For Intune administrators

Prioritize an Apple ADE runbook review where Platform SSO or Apple mobile enrollment is in scope. For Linux compliance and Edge app-protection settings, compare the revised values with the actual tenant configuration before changing baselines; the evidence does not show automatic changes to existing policies. Validate internal links after the Apple ADE page reorganization. No deadline or migration action is evidenced.
