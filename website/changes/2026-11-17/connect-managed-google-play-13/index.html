Connect Managed Google Play

17 November 2026Feature updateAction required+15 / −4 lines

In brief

The guide now identifies the Managed Google Play enterprise and Intune tenant involved, requires the Google Workspace Super Admin role for upgrades, and adds domain, account, and policy preparation for device authentication.

What Intune admins need to know

Admins performing an upgrade must ensure Super Admin access. Those requiring Google Workspace authentication must verify the domain, create managed accounts, and configure the appropriate policy.

  1. Sign in to the Microsoft Intune admin center with an Intune Administrator account.

  2. Go to Devices > Enrollment.

    After a successful upgrade, the Upgrade button appears grayed out because no more upgrades are available. If the button appears grayed out and you haven't upgraded, check your RBAC permissions.

Prepare for Google Workspace authentication on devices

The enterprise upgrade and device-user authentication are separate tasks. To require Google Workspace authentication on supported Android Enterprise devices, also prepare the following:

  • A verified Google Workspace domain.
  • Managed Google Accounts for users in the enterprise's Google Workspace domain. If needed, create accounts by following the Domain verified instructions in Add an account for a new user.

Then configure Google Workspace authentication with a settings catalog policy for supported corporate-owned devices. For personally owned devices with a work profile, use a device configuration template. The policy doesn't change the Microsoft Entra account associated with the managed device.

Disconnect your Android Enterprise administrative account

You can disconnect the link between Microsoft Intune and Google in the admin center. Disconnecting the account disables Android Enterprise device management for your tenant.
