Week in brief

Intune registry inventory guidance flags sensitive configuration exposure and access risk

This was a quiet Intune documentation week. The substantive update to Collect Device Properties clarifies that registry key inventory uses existing Device Inventory permissions and may expose sensitive device configuration information. Microsoft identifies this as an accepted risk and calls for reviewing its security and privacy implications. Two updates to Manage devices with endpoint security in Microsoft Intune only changed the page metadata date from August 2, 2024, to August 10, 2026; they show no substantive content or product change.
