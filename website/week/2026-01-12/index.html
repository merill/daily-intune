Week in brief

Windows servicing guidance is rebuilt around focused policies and staged operations

The week recorded 232 changes, dominated by a task-focused restructuring of Windows update documentation: update-ring settings, driver updates, feature updates, quality updates, Hotpatch, expedited updates, reports, and rollout options. Supporting changes refresh Edge for Business data-security guidance, Defender compliance integration, centralized admin tasks, and Autopilot known issues.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Update-ring settings are documented in a detailed new reference

    The new reference covers delivery, driver, deferral, restart, notification, pause, scan-access, and deadline controls with CSP mappings. It turns update-ring configuration into a clearer review checklist for production policies.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Hotpatch and expedited quality updates receive dedicated operational guidance

    The week separates Hotpatch and expedited quality updates from general policy documentation, reinforcing that they have distinct eligibility, urgency, rollout, and monitoring considerations.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Windows update policy content moves to task-specific pages

    New driver-update, feature-update, and update-ring policy guides replace earlier broad pages, making the latest links and page names important for operational documentation and help-desk references.

  • Intune · App management
    Edge for Business data-security guidance is renumbered and consolidated

    The documentation sequence gains a revised troubleshooting step and retires older security-baseline and end-user-experience pages, requiring any internally shared guide links to be checked.

  • Windows Autopilot · Device enrollment
    A local Autopilot Reset policy conflict is documented

    A Deny access to this computer from the network setting for the Local account prevents local administrators from initiating Autopilot Reset. Exclude affected devices or remove the setting where that reset path is required.

For Intune administrators

Make the new focused policy guides the source of truth for Windows servicing runbooks. Validate update-ring deferrals and restart controls, use feature-update policy for version targeting, reserve expedited policies for emergencies, and stage Hotpatch or driver rollouts. Also address the documented local Autopilot Reset limitation before relying on local-admin reset procedures.
