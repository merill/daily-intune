Plan for Change: Intune moving to support iOS/iPadOS 18 and higher later this year

Message CenterMC1454371 on mc.merill.net ↗Major updatePlan for change
App management

Microsoft Intune will require iOS/iPadOS 18 or higher after Apple releases iOS/iPadOS 27 later this year. Organizations should check device compatibility and Intune reports to identify affected devices. Userless devices via Automated Device Enrollment have specific OS version requirements. Use Intune controls to manage OS versions.

Plan for Change: Intune moving to support macOS 15 and higher later this year

Message CenterMC1454370 on mc.merill.net ↗Major updatePlan for change
Device enrollment

Intune will support macOS 15 and later after macOS Golden Gate 27 release this year. Existing devices on macOS 14.x or below remain enrolled but new enrollments on these versions won't be allowed. Organizations should identify and upgrade affected macOS devices in Intune before the change.
