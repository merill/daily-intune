Plan for Change: Update your iOS/iPadOS Company Portal minimum version to v5.2608.0

Message CenterMC1547994 on mc.merill.net ↗Plan for change

Starting early December, the minimum iOS/iPadOS Company Portal version will be v5.2608.0 to support new Apple OS releases. Most users update automatically; others will be prompted.
