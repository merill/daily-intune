4 November 2026Doc update+3 / −0 lines

In brief

The reference now includes Elevate UC for Intune, eScription One, and Prisma Browser for Intune, with descriptions, icons, and an Android Google Play link for eScription One.

What Intune admins need to know

Administrators can use the updated reference when reviewing protected apps for their Intune deployments.

This summary was assembled from the tracked documentation change. Verify important details in the full Microsoft Learn article.
