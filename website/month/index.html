Month in brief

Intune roadmap adds Apple OS 27 inventory, MEFERI OEMConfig, and protections

October was a quiet Intune period with two updated pages. The substantive change expands the In Development list with planned Apple OS 27 DDM inventory data, MEFERI OEMConfig support for Android Enterprise, planned removal of legacy Apple MDM software-update workloads, and two Administrator protection settings for Windows 11 24H2 and 25H2. The Managed Home Screen page also received metadata and wording changes for its app-exclusion silence setting.

  • Intune · General

    The page now lists Apple OS 27 DDM inventory data, MEFERI OEMConfig support for Android Enterprise, removal of legacy Apple MDM software-update workloads, and two Administrator protection settings for Windows 11 24H2 and 25H2.

  • Intune · App management
    Managed Home Screen revises app-exclusion silence guidance

    The page’s authoring metadata changed, and the description for excluding apps from the silence setting was revised.

For Intune administrators

Administrators should track the planned Apple, Android Enterprise, and Windows changes; no action or deadline is stated. Managed Home Screen administrators should review the revised exclusion description when configuring the silence setting.
