Day in brief

Update ring controls receive a much clearer policy reference

The day’s 60 changes are concentrated in Windows update documentation. A new update-ring settings reference spells out the configuration choices for update delivery, restart behavior, and deadlines, while companion feature, quality, reporting, and Autopilot material is reorganized around the newer policy structure.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Update-ring policy settings get a comprehensive reference

    The new reference maps update-ring settings to their Windows CSPs and explains controls for Microsoft product updates, drivers, feature and quality deferrals, Windows 11 upgrade, uninstall periods, Insider builds, notifications, restart behavior, and enforcement deadlines.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Deadline and restart settings are framed as an end-user-experience choice

    The guidance documents the available install modes and the interaction of active hours, feature and quality deadlines, grace periods, and automatic reboot before the deadline. It recommends enabling pre-deadline automatic reboot to reduce the chance of a forced restart during active hours.

  • Windows Autopilot · Device enrollment
    Autopilot known issues are refreshed alongside the update-policy work

    The Windows Autopilot known-issues documentation was updated as part of the broader refresh, giving administrators a current reference to consult before treating enrollment or reset behavior as a policy failure.
