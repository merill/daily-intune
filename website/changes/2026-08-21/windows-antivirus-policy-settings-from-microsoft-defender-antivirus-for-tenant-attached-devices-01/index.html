Windows Antivirus policy settings from Microsoft Defender Antivirus for tenant attached devices

21 August 2026Feature update+12 / −10 lines

In brief

The documented default for catch-up full scans changed to disabled, while catch-up quick scans remain enabled by default. The settings now specify that catch-up scans occur after two consecutive missed scheduled scans and require a configured scheduled scan.

What Intune admins need to know

Review Defender Antivirus policies, especially full-scan settings, because Not Configured now disables catch-up full scans. No immediate administrator action is stated.

This summary was assembled from the tracked documentation change. Verify important details in the full Microsoft Learn article.
