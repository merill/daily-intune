Day in brief

Edge for Business guidance separates enrollment policy paths and adds data-security troubleshooting

The period was mostly documentation maintenance—terminology, links, formatting, and page restructuring—but several changes have direct administrative value. Secure Enterprise Browser guidance now distinguishes policy paths by enrollment status and adds related troubleshooting material, while Windows update guidance sharpens rollout timing and introduces driver-policy procedures. Azure Monitor guidance also adds a concrete expectation for Intune data availability.

  • Intune · App management
    Edge for Business policy guidance now distinguishes enrolled and non-enrolled clients

    The revised Secure Enterprise Browser sequence maps App Configuration Policies to non-enrolled devices and Settings Catalog policies to enrolled devices. It removes the Security Baseline deployment step from the sequence, says enrolled Windows devices should use either Settings Catalog or the Microsoft Edge Security Baseline—not both—and warns against targeting one client with both App Configuration and Settings Catalog policies.

  • Intune · App management
    A new Edge for Business step adds data-security troubleshooting coverage

    The new Step 7 covers policy assignments, settings, sign-in, app and platform requirements, configuration channels, policy conflicts, and platform-specific issues. It gives administrators checks and resolutions for diagnosing app protection and app configuration policy deployment or enforcement problems.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Feature-update rollout guidance separates availability from installation timing

    The revised rollout article explains three availability behaviors, offer groups, and rollout scheduling, while distinguishing when an update is offered from when installation occurs. Installation timing depends on controls such as deadlines and restart settings, giving administrators clearer guidance for gradual deployments.

  • Intune · Device updates
    Windows driver update guidance adds approval, pause, and deployment procedures

    The new how-to covers prerequisites, automatic or manual driver approval, pausing and reapproving updates, and deployment behavior. It advises checking that update-ring and Settings Catalog policies allow driver installation and avoiding assignments that place devices in multiple driver update policies.

  • Intune · Endpoint analytics
    Azure Monitor guidance sets a 24-hour cadence and 48-hour data window

    The updated guidance states that Device Compliance Organizational Logs and Intune Devices report data is sent once every 24 hours, with export occurring during each 24-hour period and taking up to 48 hours overall. This sets the expected wait when investigating missing data.
