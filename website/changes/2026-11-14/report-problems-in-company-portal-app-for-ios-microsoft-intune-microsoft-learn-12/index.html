Report Problems in Company Portal App for iOS - Microsoft Intune | Microsoft Learn

14 November 2026Doc update+11 / −11 lines

In brief

The documentation updates capitalization, interface terminology such as “tap” to “select,” grammar, and the publication date while retaining the same reporting and log-collection steps.

What Intune admins need to know

No administrator action is required; the guidance is clearer for users reporting Company Portal issues.

Report problemsProblems in Company Portal appApp for iOS - Microsoft Intune | Microsoft Learn

Applies to Intune Company Portal app for iOS/iPadOS

Access help and support in app

You can access the reporting feature in Company Portal by using any of these methods:

  • When you receive an error message or alert, tapselect Report.
  • Under the More tab ofIn the Company Portal app, tapunder More, select Send Logs.
  • In the Company Portal app, shake your device, and then tapselect Send Diagnostic Report. If the diagnostics report prompt doesn't appear when you shake the device, open the Settings and go to Apps. Then go to Company Portal, and turn on Shake Gesture.

Share diagnostic logs

  1. Try to reproduce the event you experienced. This step is optional but ensures that the Company Portal details appear at the top of the logs when uploaded.

  2. Use one of the following methods to initiate the upload:

    • When you receive an error message, tapselect Report.
    • Shake your device. Then tapselect Send diagnostic report.

  3. Wait while Company Portal uploads the logs from Company Portal. When that's done, tapselect Open Authenticator to collect logs from the Microsoft Authenticator app.

  4. Save the incident ID for your records and to share with your support person. The incident ID is unique to your report.

  5. Select Email Logs to follow up with your support person with more details so they can get started with your case. In the body of the email, explain what you experienced and include your incident ID. Your support person maymight use the incident ID to enlist help from Microsoft about your case.

Still need help? Contact your support person. For contact information, check the Company Portal website. \ No newline at end of file
